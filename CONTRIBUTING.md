# Contributing

Corrections, stronger sources and clearly dated updates are welcome. The repository is written in English for readers unfamiliar with cybersecurity and Argentine institutions.

## Propose a correction

Open an issue or pull request identifying:

1. The file and statement concerned.
2. The proposed wording.
3. A direct public source link, its publication date, and the date of the underlying event where known.
4. Whether the source is an institutional statement, direct investigation, other reporting, commentary, or an unverified claim.
5. Whether the change corrects the October 9, 2026 snapshot or adds information published later.

Institutional statements and criminal allegations must remain attributed. A complaint, investigation or arrest is not a final court judgment. A seller's claimed record count does not establish a victim count.

## Preserve the evidence standard

- Keep confirmed disclosure, acknowledged incident, disputed allegation and enforcement development distinct.
- Separate intrusion, discovery, publication and response dates.
- Identify recycled records and overlapping campaigns where the evidence allows it.
- Explain necessary technical terms in ordinary language.
- Cite the page that supports the claim, preferably an original statement or direct investigation.
- Treat harms as documented outcomes only when supported by case-specific evidence; otherwise label them as risks or analysis.
- Do not infer national economic losses, population-wide exposure or departmental rankings from an incomplete sample.

## Keep the documents consistent

`REPORT.md` is the complete narrative. The topic files in `docs/` reproduce its sections for easier navigation. Update both when a finding changes, and update `docs/SOURCES.md` if a reference changes.

Record substantive changes in `CHANGELOG.md`. Later evidence should have its own date and should not silently rewrite what was known at the original cutoff.

## What to submit

Submit original explanatory text and public source references. Do not attach leaked datasets, personal victim details, credentials or copies of entire third-party articles. If a source contains sensitive records, link to appropriate reporting or an institutional notice rather than to the exposed records.

Contributions to the original research text are submitted under the repository's [CC BY 4.0 license](LICENSE.md). External sources retain their own rights.
