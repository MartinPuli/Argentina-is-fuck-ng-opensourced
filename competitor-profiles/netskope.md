# Netskope — controls over sensitive data movement

**Snapshot:** October 9, 2026 · **Depth:** targeted capability scan
**Primary source:** [Official page](https://www.netskope.com/solutions/public-sector)

## Verified source statement

Vendor-described capabilities. Netskope markets data-loss prevention for public-sector environments: identifying sensitive content and enforcing controls over transfers to cloud and web destinations. This page alone does not identify a particular government deployment or prove a prevention rate.

## Boundary and evidence strength

Data-loss prevention means controls intended to stop sensitive information leaving through monitored channels. It complements access control but depends on what traffic and content the deployment can inspect.

## Implication for BREACHSTOP

Use existing data-movement controls where available. Our prototype should prove a narrow application boundary rather than claim it replaces an enterprise data-protection platform. Test alternate paths and allowed business exports explicitly.
