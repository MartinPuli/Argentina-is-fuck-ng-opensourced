# Wazuh — reusable open-source response building block

**Snapshot:** October 9, 2026 · **Depth:** targeted capability scan
**Primary source:** [Official page](https://documentation.wazuh.com/current/user-manual/capabilities/active-response/)

## Verified source statement

Official product documentation describes responses triggered by configured alerts, including stateful actions that expire and stateless actions that persist. Its default-script documentation includes account disabling and network blocking.

## Boundary and evidence strength

Relevant as a possible connector or response executor, rather than a country-specific deployment claim. We did not install, audit or benchmark Wazuh during this comparison.

## Implication for BREACHSTOP

Do not rebuild endpoint collection solely to make an AI demo. Integrate a narrow, verified response adapter later. Keep the agent's ability to propose a response separate from the administrator's policy that permits execution.

## Coverage limits

Pricing, commercial licensing terms, SEO metrics, reviews and market share were not collected. Firecrawl and DataForSEO tools were unavailable; official pages were retrieved with the browsing tool. This is a technical comparison, not a purchasing recommendation or a full competitive audit.
